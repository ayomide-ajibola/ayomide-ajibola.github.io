01. About Me

I hold a Master's of Science and Bachelor's of Science in Computational Science & Engineering from Kean University (GPA: 3.5/4.0). My academic and research career has centered on the intersection of mathematical computing, high-throughput genomic data, and intelligent systems.

For my Master's Thesis, directed by Dr. Bin Hu, I explored the semantic boundaries of Large Language Models on physical kinetic sensing. 03. Featured Projects

Machine Learning & LLM • Master's Thesis

Multimodal Sensor Analytics & Hybrid LLM Reasoning Engine

Master's thesis project evaluating Human Activity Recognition on the HARTH benchmark dataset (720,639 samples across 12 physical classes). Extracted sliding-window time/frequency statistical features, benchmarked 1D-CNN, LSTM, and classical models, and developed an innovative soft-voting ensemble uniting SVM probability distributions with few-shot quantized LLMs (Phi-3-Mini, Gemma-2B) to reach 96% accuracy. Includes automated clinical narrative summarization of longitudinal activity logs.
